# Global Video Game Sales Analysis

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel · Data Analysis · Business Intelligence · Dashboarding

## Project Overview

The video game industry has evolved significantly across platforms, genres, publishers, and geographic markets. Understanding historical sales patterns can help identify which types of games have performed strongly, which publishers have dominated the market, and how demand has varied across regions and over time.

This project analyzes historical global video game sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to uncover patterns across **genres, publishers, regions, platforms, and release years**.

The analysis focuses on four key questions:

1. Which video game genres generate the highest global sales?
2. Which publishers account for the largest share of recorded sales?
3. How have video game sales changed over time?
4. How does sales performance differ across geographic markets?

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to make these patterns easier to explore and communicate.

## Business Problem

Video game performance varies considerably depending on genre, publisher, platform, release period, and geographic market.

Without analyzing these dimensions together, it can be difficult to identify where historical demand has been concentrated and which segments have consistently generated strong sales.

The objective of this analysis was to transform raw historical sales records into insights that could support questions around:

- Product and genre performance
- Publisher performance
- Geographic market differences
- Historical market trends
- Portfolio and market prioritization

# Executive Summary

The analysis identified several clear patterns in historical video game sales.

**Action** games generated the largest cumulative sales among the genres analyzed, followed by **Sports** games.

**Nintendo** recorded the highest publisher-level sales in the dataset, supported by several high-performing titles.

Geographically, **North America** accounted for the largest recorded regional sales total.

Sales also increased substantially through the 1990s and early 2000s before reaching their highest annual level around **2008**, followed by a decline in the recorded dataset.

These results show that historical video game sales have been highly concentrated across particular genres, publishers, titles, and geographic markets.

# Analysis & Findings

## 1. Sales Performance Over Time

The store's total recorded sales reached **$8,920.44M**, with the highest annual level occurring around **2008**, when recorded sales reached approximately **$678M**.

Historical sales increased considerably through the 1990s and early 2000s before declining after the 2008 peak.

![Sales Trends](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b028d904-fba5-4e9f-b1db-fc7fb7f3bdb5)

The dataset demonstrates the historical pattern clearly, but the available variables do not by themselves establish the cause of the decline.

## 2. Genre Performance

Genre analysis shows a strong concentration of sales among a small number of categories.

**Action** was the highest-selling genre, generating approximately **$1,751M** in cumulative recorded sales. **Sports** followed with approximately **$1,331M**.

Shooter and Role-Playing games also recorded substantial sales, while categories such as Puzzle and Adventure accounted for smaller portions of the overall total.

![Genre Analysis](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e7d4990e-c145-46de-8b10-8fb6807ff2cf)

![Genre Analysis](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/41278149-4ca7-4c01-b047-dc02c5071fdc)

This indicates that historical demand in the dataset was not evenly distributed across genres.

## 3. Publisher Performance

Publisher-level analysis reveals substantial concentration among major publishers.

**Nintendo** recorded approximately **$1,781M** in cumulative sales, making it the highest-selling publisher represented in the analysis.

**Electronic Arts** followed with approximately **$1,110M**, while **Activision** recorded approximately **$727M**.

![Publisher Analysis](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6de28123-9bf6-46cc-a7a9-e2a2c85dd7d4)

![Publisher Analysis](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4855322d-c143-4363-b558-da491f2632db)

Nintendo's performance is supported by several exceptionally high-selling titles, including **Wii Sports**.

The results demonstrate how a relatively small group of major publishers accounts for a significant share of the historical sales represented in the dataset.

## 4. Regional Market Performance

Sales patterns vary considerably across geographic markets.

**North America** was the largest market represented in the dataset, accounting for approximately **$4,392.95M** in recorded sales.

Europe was another major market, while Japan displayed different patterns of genre and title performance compared with Western markets.

![Regional Performance](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a17c57bf-f0fd-4e14-95bf-83431ad4d94a)

The regional differences highlight the importance of evaluating market performance geographically rather than assuming that globally successful games perform equally across all regions.

# Business Recommendations

**Prioritize historically strong genres while maintaining portfolio diversity.** Action and Sports generated particularly strong cumulative sales. These categories can receive greater attention when evaluating historical product performance, while other genres remain relevant for differentiated audience segments.

**Evaluate geographic markets independently.** North America represents the largest recorded market, but regional preferences differ. Product and market strategies should therefore consider regional performance rather than relying solely on global totals.

**Use publisher and title-level analysis when evaluating portfolio opportunities.** The strong concentration of sales among publishers such as Nintendo, Electronic Arts, and Activision demonstrates the importance of examining both publisher performance and individual high-performing franchises.

**Combine historical sales with current market data before making forward-looking decisions.** Historical performance provides useful context but should be supplemented with more recent information such as pricing, platform adoption, customer behavior, digital distribution, and current market trends.

# Limitations

This analysis should be interpreted as a **historical exploratory analysis**, rather than a complete representation of the current video game market.

- The dataset contains aggregated game-level information rather than individual customer transactions.
- Customer demographics are not available.
- Pricing information is not available.
- Marketing expenditure and acquisition channels are not included.
- Digital distribution and subscription behavior cannot be directly measured.
- Historical relationships do not necessarily represent current gaming-market conditions.
- The dataset cannot establish the causes of changes in sales over time.

These limitations mean that the analysis is most useful for understanding **historical patterns and relationships** rather than predicting current consumer behavior.
